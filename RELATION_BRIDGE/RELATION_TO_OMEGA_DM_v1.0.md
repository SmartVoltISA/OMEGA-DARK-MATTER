## Purpose

This document connects the experimentally studied relation-first mechanisms in `SmartVoltISA/RELATION-LAB` with the hypothesis space of `SmartVoltISA/OMEGA-DARK-MATTER`.

## What is already established inside Omega-DM

1. Synthetic identifiability can recover a hidden relational contribution, but Ω-DM-001 used an inverse-square generator and therefore does not derive gravity.
2. Ω-DM-002 through Ω-DM-005 support predictive information in hidden relational structure under controlled synthetic models.
3. Ω-DM-006 showed that naive fixed coupling does not scale safely with system size; future models must normalize coupling by local degree/density.
4. Ω-DM-007 showed attraction can be produced by relational dynamics without explicitly writing an inverse-square force, but the closed two-body model has scale-identifiability limits.
5. Ω-DM-008 showed that a 3D local Poisson equation produces an inverse-square gradient. This is a mathematical mechanism, not evidence for physical dark matter.
6. Ω-DM-009..011 showed that simple scalar source/inertia equivalences are construction-dependent and can fail under independent observables or scaling changes.
7. Ω-DM-012 showed that a purely pairwise velocity-difference kinetic action has a global translation zero mode and therefore cannot by itself reproduce ordinary uniform translational inertia.

## Critical gap

The missing bridge is an independently generated, relational-only system in which:

- no mass field is inserted;
- no 1/r^2 force is inserted;
- no Poisson equation is imposed as the target law;
- localized structures arise from internal relational dynamics;
- an effective inertial parameter is measured from response/energy/impulse;
- two separated structures generate a long-range response;
- the spatial falloff is measured blindly;
- the result is compared against Newtonian 1/r^2, Yukawa/exponential, and alternative power laws;
- source strength and effective inertia are varied independently;
- coordinate/discretization/partition invariance is checked;
- randomized and shuffled relational nulls are mandatory.

## Next experiment family

### Ω-DM-013: Emergent relational inertia

Use a relational field with internal state q and edge coupling plus a local bounded state potential. Measure a moving localized defect's effective kinetic coefficient from independent trajectories.

Candidate discrete energy:

T = 0.5 * sum_i(qdot_i^2)
V = 0.5 * sum_(i,j) W_ij * (q_i-q_j)^2 + sum_i lambda/4 * (q_i^2-1)^2

The localized structure coordinate X is NOT assigned a mass term. Its effective inertia is inferred from the field response.

### Ω-DM-014: Interaction between emergent structures

Create two independently generated localized structures. Measure force/acceleration from the change of total energy with separation. Fit the blind family:

F(r) = A/r^p
F(r) = A*exp(-r/L)
F(r) = A/(r^2 + r0^2)^(p/2)

The law and fitting window must be preregistered before the final run.

### Ω-DM-015: 3D relational medium / far-field test

Extend the emergent structures to 3D with degree-normalized coupling. Measure the far-field response outside the source core. The primary question is whether a stable long-range regime emerges and what exponent p is recovered without encoding p=2.

### Ω-DM-016: Source-inertia equivalence test

Independently vary source structure and emergent inertial structure. Test whether the far-field amplitude tracks an independently measured effective source/inertia quantity. Ω-DM-011 showed that naive scalar equivalence fails; this experiment must use response-defined quantities instead.

## Acceptance discipline

A result may be called SUPPORT only if it survives:

- independent generator/observable separation;
- negative control;
- parameter perturbation;
- randomized relational null;
- finite-size scaling;
- coordinate/discretization checks;
- blind model comparison;
- preregistered thresholds.

Otherwise use EXPLORATORY or INCONCLUSIVE.

## Interpretation rule

Relation-Lab is the mechanism library. Ω-DM is the physical hypothesis test. A successful Relation-Lab experiment is evidence that a mechanism exists in its model; it becomes evidence for Ω-DM only after an explicit transfer experiment with physical target observables and independent controls.
