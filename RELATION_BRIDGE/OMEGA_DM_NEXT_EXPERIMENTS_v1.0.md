# Ω-DM NEXT EXPERIMENTS v1.0

## Goal

Move from relational mechanisms already demonstrated in RELATION-LAB to a non-circular dark-matter-relevant test.

## Priority order

1. Ω-DM-013 — emergent inertia of a localized relational structure.
2. Ω-DM-014 — interaction/far-field law between two emergent structures.
3. Ω-DM-015 — 3D scaling and blind falloff exponent.
4. Ω-DM-016 — independent source/inertia equivalence.

## Hard exclusions

Do not insert:

- inverse-square force;
- Newtonian mass as a hidden parameter;
- Poisson source chosen to reproduce the target law;
- target exponent p=2 in the generator;
- fitted observable reused as the source observable.

## Required controls

- paired identical-noise controls where applicable;
- randomized relation topology;
- shuffled source/response labels;
- positive control;
- negative control;
- finite-size scaling;
- coordinate transformation;
- partition/discretization change;
- blind comparison of power-law and exponential families;
- preregistered acceptance thresholds.

## Decision vocabulary

PASS = preregistered criterion met.
FAIL = preregistered criterion not met.
INCONCLUSIVE = experiment exposes a model or identifiability limitation that prevents the intended inference.
EXPLORATORY = mechanism observed but not yet suitable as evidence for Ω-DM.

## Core rule

No mechanism is promoted from RELATION-LAB to physical evidence merely because it reproduces a familiar law. The law must emerge in an independently defined observable and survive the nulls and robustness checks.
