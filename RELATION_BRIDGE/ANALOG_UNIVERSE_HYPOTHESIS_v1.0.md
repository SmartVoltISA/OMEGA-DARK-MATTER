# ANALOG UNIVERSE HYPOTHESIS v1.0

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: OPEN HYPOTHESIS — NOT ESTABLISHED

## Statement

Working hypothesis:

The physical Universe may be describable as a continuous dynamical relational medium in which geometry, temporal behavior, localization, inertia and interaction are effective properties of evolving relations rather than primitive independent objects.

The word `analog` is used here operationally to mean that relevant state variables and relational responses possess continuous degrees of freedom and evolve through continuous or effectively continuous dynamics. It is NOT yet a claim about the ontology of the real Universe.

## Why this hypothesis is being investigated

RELATION-LAB provides model-bounded evidence that:

- controlled interventions can reveal directional dependence;
- propagation responses can encode recoverable spatial structure;
- anisotropic and nonreciprocal responses can induce directional geometry;
- geometry can survive nontrivial coordinate transformations as an invariant predictive structure;
- nonlinear relational dynamics can support self-confined localized states;
- localized states can possess reproducible internal eigenmodes;
- hidden relational structure can sometimes be reconstructed from response fingerprints.

These are not proofs of emergent physical spacetime. They define mechanisms that can be transferred into stricter falsification tests.

## Proposed hierarchy

`DISTINCTION -> RELATION -> STATE -> DYNAMICS -> RESPONSE -> GEOMETRY -> STRUCTURE -> INERTIA -> INTERACTION -> OBSERVABLE`

The research direction reverses the conventional modelling order: instead of assuming objects and geometry first, infer effective objects and geometry from reproducible dynamical responses.

## Required tests for the physical claim

The hypothesis must eventually survive independent tests of:

1. continuity versus discreteness;
2. locality;
3. finite propagation speed;
4. memory/history;
5. coordinate invariance;
6. dimensional scaling;
7. stable localized structures;
8. effective inertia;
9. interaction laws;
10. energy/momentum conservation or the appropriate generalized conservation laws;
11. Lorentz-like or other observed symmetry constraints;
12. gravitational observations;
13. quantum observations;
14. cosmological observations.

## Falsification

The hypothesis is weakened if the required phenomena cannot be produced without inserting the target laws explicitly, if results depend irreducibly on discretization or coordinate choices, or if independent physical observations contradict the model.

## Epistemic boundary

The present research does not establish that:

- the Universe is analog;
- space is emergent;
- time is emergent;
- matter is emergent;
- gravity is relational;
- dark matter is relational structure.

Those are future claims requiring future evidence.

## Relation to Ω-DM

Ω-DM is one focused branch of this larger hypothesis. It asks whether hidden relational structure can account for some gravitational effects currently attributed to dark matter.

The stronger foundational hypothesis is therefore tested first at the mechanism level, then at the gravitational level, and only later against broader physical observations.

## Rule

UNKNOWN != TRUE

OBSERVATION != INTERPRETATION != HYPOTHESIS != RESULT != PROOF

A mechanism demonstrated in a synthetic model remains a mechanism demonstrated in that model until independently connected to physical evidence.
