# Ω-DM-024 — Conservation → Geometry Across Dimension

## Question
Is the scaling exponent determined by conservation plus geometric shell growth, rather than by a specific Laplacian form?

## Hypothesis
For a localized conserved relational flux Q(r), the radial response gradient should scale as G(r) ~ Q(r)/S_d(r), where S_d(r) is the measure of a shell in dimension d. Therefore, for d=1,2,3, p should approach d-1.

## Tests
A. Analytic geometry test: construct conserved shell flux without any radial inverse-power law in the response generator; recover p from shell measure only.
B. Structural null: deliberately introduce radius-dependent leakage Q(r) and verify that the d-1 law is lost.
C. Dimension comparison: repeat over d=1,2,3 and six independent perturbations.

## Restrictions
No inverse-square response is inserted. No gravitational force, G, dark matter, or dimension-specific radial kernel is used. The primary object is conserved relational flux and its shell geometry.

## Decision
PASS only if the conserved case recovers p≈d-1 across d=1,2,3 and the leakage control measurably violates the prediction.

## Boundary
This is a structural mathematical test. It cannot establish physical gravity or dark matter.
