# Ω-DM-026 — RESULT-001

**Status: INCONCLUSIVE / H0 RETAINED**

## Actual local execution
Environment: Python + SciPy 1.17.0. Six independent random irregular 3D point clouds were tested with a Delaunay local graph. Pair exchange was nonlinear and antisymmetric:
`J_ij = w_ij*tanh(alpha*(u_i-u_j))/alpha`.
No inverse-square law or gravitational term was inserted.

### Moderate source amplitude: A=5, alpha=2
Measured gradient exponents p=q+1:

- seed 0: 2.744
- seed 1: 3.063
- seed 2: 3.113
- seed 3: 3.155
- seed 4: 3.082
- seed 5: 2.985
- mean: 3.024
- sample SD: 0.148

Direct spherical-cut flux was conserved to numerical precision in these runs (CV effectively 0 at displayed precision), showing that antisymmetric local exchange preserves total source flux.

### Stronger nonlinear stress test
For A=50, the first three seeds gave p=2.129, 1.540, 3.220 and shell-flux CV about 0.75–0.97. For A=200 they gave p=5.696, 6.661, 6.427 with similarly large flux variation. The nonlinear regime therefore does not produce a stable p=2 law here.

A=5 was already enough to show that irregular geometry plus local nonlinear exchange does not generically preserve the earlier lattice p≈2 result.

## Interpretation
This run **does not support H1**. The earlier inverse-square-like behavior is not universal under this broader model class. The experiment is therefore retained as **INCONCLUSIVE/H0**, not a failure of the broader Ω-DM program.

The result is scientifically useful: conservation from antisymmetric pair exchange alone is insufficient to determine the radial scaling exponent. Geometry, connectivity and constitutive response matter.

## Scope / non-claim
Nothing here derives physical gravity or dark matter. These are model-level relational dynamics only. In particular, the observed conserved flux is not evidence that gravitational flux is physically generated by relational exchange.
