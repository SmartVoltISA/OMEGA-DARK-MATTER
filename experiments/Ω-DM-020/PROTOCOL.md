# Ω-DM-020 — Dimensionality of Relational Environmental Response

## Question

Does the radial structure of an environmental relational response depend systematically on the dimensionality of the surrounding relation system?

The target is the environmental response, not an assumed force or entity.

## Design

Use the same class of local symmetric positive relational operators in 1D, 2D and 3D. A localized intervention is applied at the center. Measure the shell-averaged environmental difference relative to the zero-intervention control and its radial gradient.

No inverse-square law is inserted.

## Predeclared expectation

For a genuinely isotropic scale-free Green-function-like response generated by local conservation/balance, the radial gradient should scale approximately as:

1D: r^0
2D: r^-1
3D: r^-2

These are predictions to test, not assumptions used to fit a single dimension.

## Controls

- multiple independent random relation realizations per dimension
- source-location permutation
- relation-weight permutation
- resolution scaling
- interior-range stability

## Decision

H1 is supported only if the fitted gradient exponents track the dimensional prediction across all three dimensions with stable confidence intervals and controls do not reproduce the structure.

A failure in any dimension is retained as a model-level falsification/inconclusive result; it is not converted into a gravity claim.

## Important limitation

Finite lattices, boundaries and random anisotropy can bias shell averages. The experiment must therefore distinguish dimensional scaling from finite-size artifacts before interpreting the exponent.
