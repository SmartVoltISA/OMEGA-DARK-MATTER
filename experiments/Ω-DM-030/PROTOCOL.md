# Ω-DM-030 — Emergent geometry from relation-only growth

## Question
Can an effective 3D shell-growth law S(r) ~ r^2 arise from a graph generated without embedding nodes in coordinates and without prescribing a 3D lattice?

## H1
A relation-only local growth rule, with no coordinate geometry supplied, generically produces an intrinsic shell-growth exponent near 2.

## H0
The tested coordinate-free relational growth rules do not robustly generate an intrinsic 3D shell-growth exponent; additional geometric/metric structure is required.

## Construction
Three coordinate-free graph generators are tested: preferential local attachment with triadic closure, fixed-degree random regular relational networks, and preferential attachment networks.

Analysis uses only graph adjacency and shortest-path distance from a source node. No node coordinates are used.

## Measurement
Count nodes in graph-distance shells C(r). Fit log C(r) versus log r over the finite interior range. Six independent seeds are used for each generator.

## Acceptance
H1 requires a stable exponent near 2 with small seed-to-seed variation across the relation-only class. Systematic departure or strong model dependence retains H0.

## Scope
This is a structural test only; it cannot establish physical spacetime or gravity. A negative result identifies missing conditions for emergent geometry.
