## Result

The localized relational defect has a strictly positive effective kinetic coefficient even though no mass parameter and no absolute translational kinetic term were introduced.

Certified energy-metric runs:

- N=101, k=0.5: M_E approximately 1.203
- N=101, k=1.0: M_E approximately 0.913
- N=101, k=2.0: M_E approximately 0.657
- N=201, k=0.5: M_E approximately 1.203
- N=201, k=1.0: M_E approximately 0.913
- N=201, k=2.0: M_E approximately 0.657
- N=401, k=0.5: M_E approximately 1.203
- N=401, k=1.0: M_E approximately 0.913
- N=401, k=2.0: M_E approximately 0.657

All 9 primary runs produced M_E > 0.

The coefficient changes systematically with stiffness: increasing k from 0.5 to 2.0 reduces M_E from about 1.203 to about 0.657 for the same defect family. This is not a manually inserted mass scaling; it follows from the internal shape of the relaxed relational excitation.

Velocity scaling U={0.1,0.2,0.4} reproduces the same inferred M_E by construction of the collective-coordinate energy measurement, providing a consistency control rather than independent dynamical evidence.

## Scientific interpretation

This is the first positive result in the Ω-DM-012/013/014 sequence for an emergent inertial coefficient generated by internal structure rather than by an explicit mass term.

The distinction is crucial:

Ω-DM-012: uniform translation of a purely relational system is an exact null mode.
Ω-DM-013: adding internal edge dynamics without breaking the difference-only structure leaves that null mode intact.
Ω-DM-014: a localized internal relational excitation is different. Moving the excitation changes many internal field values, so its collective coordinate inherits a nonzero kinetic metric.

Therefore a mass-like coefficient can emerge as a property of a moving structural excitation, even when mass is not present as a primitive parameter.

## Important limitation

This is not yet a proof of physical inertia or gravity. Measurement A is a collective-coordinate energy metric, and the velocity-scaling check shares the same construction. The independent dynamical-response measurement (M_D) specified in the protocol has NOT yet been certified.

Accordingly the result is deliberately labelled PASS-H1 for the energy-defined emergence claim, with dynamical validation open. The next run must independently perturb the defect, measure acceleration, and compare M_D against M_E without defining one from the other.
