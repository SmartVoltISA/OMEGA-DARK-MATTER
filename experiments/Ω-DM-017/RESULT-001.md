# Ω-DM-017 — RESULT-001

Status: EXPLORATORY PASS (model-level environmental response)

## Result

For a 3D nearest-neighbour relational lattice with a localized central source condition and zero boundary conditions, the differential environmental response is spatially structured and approximately scale-free over an interior range.

Local numerical run: N=25 total grid points per axis; source at the central cell; unit source; zero boundary.

### Shell-averaged field response

Power-law fit over the interior gave approximately:

`Δu(r) ~ r^(-1.7795)`

with `R² = 0.9866`.

The field exponent is biased by finite-grid/discrete effects and is NOT the primary target for a gravity claim.

### Radial response gradient

The directly measured radial response gradient gave:

- fit range r=3..7: exponent `2.0053`, `R² = 0.99939`
- fit range r=4..9: exponent `2.0345`, `R² = 0.99973`

Thus, in this specified 3D relational medium, the environmental response gradient is very close to an inverse-square spatial law over the tested interior ranges.

## What was and was not inserted

NOT inserted:

- inverse-square force law
- gravitational potential
- gravitational constant
- mass-dependent attraction
- a pre-defined gravitational force between objects

The only ingredients were local nearest-neighbour relational coupling, a localized source condition, and boundary conditions.
