# Ω-DM-010 — RESULT-001

Date: 2026-09-11
Status: FAIL — H1 NOT SUPPORTED

## Certification outcome

The preregistered positive source/inertia relation was not reproduced in the first independent construction.

A local certification probe using 40 independently generated weighted graphs produced a correlation of approximately r = -0.997 between the defined source response and the independently defined inertial-response quantity.

The sign is opposite to the preregistered H1 expectation of a positive common scale.

## Interpretation

This is a useful negative result.

The experiment does NOT support the claim that an arbitrary relational graph automatically produces one common scalar behaving simultaneously as source strength and inertial mass.

The construction instead exposed an inverse relationship between the particular source-response and stiffness-derived inertia observables.

This means the previous Ω-DM-009 source/inertia agreement cannot be generalized without identifying the dynamical principle that makes the two quantities share a scale.

## Consequence for the programme

We should not call the relational source quantity "mass" yet.

The next step is to identify whether a common scale can emerge from a conservation or action principle rather than from graph statistics.

## Limitations

This was a synthetic relational model, not a physical gravity simulation. The negative result falsifies only the preregistered claim for this construction; it does not falsify relational explanations of gravity in general.
